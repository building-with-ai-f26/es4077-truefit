TrueFit

Paste a job posting. Know if the start date works, then get a resume for it that's ATS-ready and 100% true.

1Check the start date

Leave blank for June 2027. Any start date in or after your month counts as in window. You can also type another month like "August 2027", or "ASAP" or "Flexible".

2Your experience

TrueFit uses what you say you did here (actions, results, skills you used) to write bullets, along with your resume and nothing else. Goals and things you hope to learn are never turned into claims.

Check the start date first.

3Tailored resume

Every bullet shows the lines it came from. Unverified bullets couldn't be traced and are left out unless you put them back.

Plain text for Workday or iCIMS: only the bullets you've kept.
